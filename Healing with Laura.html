( 03 — Laura’s story )

Born from
my own healing.

“Five years ago, my life changed drastically.”

Laura became unwell with Long COVID, and a cascade of chronic conditions followed. Most days she was bedridden, and she lost her long career in the care industry, supporting children with brain injuries.

She waited months for healthcare, and the medications she was given brought further difficulties. Her mental health suffered too.

Those years of caring for vulnerable people shaped the practitioner she is today — with deep empathy, patience and listening — and led her into her calling as a Reiki healer.
